## Corrections and unresolved facts

- Goldie’s Conch House: Maps reported temporarily closed; accessible operator evidence did not establish reopening. Withheld from the new directory. The existing homepage’s historical menu chat remains unchanged and is not evidence of current opening.
- Big Picture Paint & Sip: tourism listing still names Sandyport; Maps identifies 299 Bay Street. Withheld until the operator confirms its current location.
- Rum Bahamas Festival: no verified current edition. Withheld rather than assigning old dates to a new year.
- Heritage Museum: Graycliff and Maps report temporary closure for renovations. The public page clearly carries that notice and does not recommend an interior visit.
- Fort Fincastle: Maps reports temporary closure, while older tourism pages describe the attraction. The page says access needs confirmation and does not claim reopening.
- Paradise Beach: the official tourism board verifies the named beach on Paradise Island, but Maps searches did not establish a distinct public entrance. No confirmed Maps-match date is recorded. The page flags access uncertainty; a Maps search is not proof of an entrance. Confirm resort access before travel.
- D’Aguilar Art Foundation: now represented by V&M Gallery near Orange Hill, based on the gallery website; Maps matches V&M Art Gallery on West Bay Street. Old downtown directions are omitted.
- Doongalik: use the operator’s 20 Village Road, also matched in Maps, rather than the reference’s old number.
- Queen’s Staircase: lower and upper approaches differ; location text avoids presenting the fort approach as step-free access.
- Events: no assumed future dates, ticket prices or Event structured data. The food festival’s October 17–18, 2026 edition is described as past.

## Evidence model

`data/nassau-directory.json` records original copy, supporting URLs, claim scopes and check dates. `mapsCheckedOn` means the venue identity/location was matched in the Maps UI; it is not a guarantee of current opening. Hours, fees and booking conditions rely on operator or tourism sources rather than Maps alone. Maps reviews, descriptions and photos were not imported.

Visit durations are explicitly ShoreDay planning suggestions, not measured travel times. Exact taxi fares and unverified hours/prices are omitted. Missing accessibility information never implies access. Place pages use ordinary Google Maps links, not a paid API.
